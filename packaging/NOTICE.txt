Cupbox

This program plays video with FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), used as a
separate set of DLLs under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later. The FFmpeg license is in
LICENSE-FFmpeg-LGPLv2.1.txt next to this file.

The FFmpeg build shipped here is configured without GPL components. It
carries these containers: MP4/MOV, MKV/WebM, AVI, FLV, MPEG-TS, MPEG-PS,
ASF/WMV, OGG, WAV, MP3, FLAC and AAC (ADTS); these video decoders: H.264,
HEVC, AV1, VP9, VP8, VP6, MPEG-1/2, MPEG-4 Part 2, VC-1, WMV1/2/3,
MS-MPEG4, FLV, MJPEG and H.263; and these audio decoders: AAC, MP3, Opus,
Vorbis, FLAC, ALAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, TrueHD, WMA1/2, MP2, PCM, APE,
WavPack, AMR-NB/WB and Speex; and these subtitle decoders: ASS, SubRip,
mov_text, WebVTT, PGS, DVD and DVB subtitles. It links zlib for the
compressed subtitle tracks of Matroska files, and the MinGW pthread
runtime; zlib1.dll and libwinpthread-1.dll ship next to cupbox.exe for
that reason.

FFmpeg source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg (release/8.0)

The ending background uses imaging (https://github.com/disintegration/imaging).
